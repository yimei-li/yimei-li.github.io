I am currently exploring postdoctoral opportunities for after I finish my Ph.D. in **May 2027**. My start date is flexible, and I would be excited to discuss opportunities beginning anytime from May through December 2027. If there may be a fit, please feel free to contact me at [yimei.li@princeton.edu](mailto:yimei.li@princeton.edu).

My work focuses on viruses and their dynamics **within hosts**, **between hosts**, and at the **population level**. I use **mathematical**, **statistical**, and **machine learning** tools to study these processes. 

I have developed a series of projects centered on defective viral genomes (DVGs) across **multiple scales**, spanning **in vitro experiments**, **within-host dynamics**, **therapeutics** and **transmission**, and **network-level** processes. 

Through these projects, I have had the chance to work across different biological levels and modeling approaches, and I hope this experience will be useful in future projects on virus dynamics and infectious disease modeling. I am also interested in immuno-epidemiology, vaccination, and nonlinear dynamics across pathogens such as influenza, dengue, SARS-CoV-2, and others.

Currently, I am a Ph.D. candidate in [Quantitative and Computational Biology](https://lsi.princeton.edu/qcb-graduate-program) at Princeton University, affiliated with the [Lewis-Sigler Institute for Integrative Genomics](https://lsi.princeton.edu/). I am advised by [Bryan Grenfell](https://eeb.princeton.edu/people/bryan-grenfell), [Simon A. Levin](https://eeb.princeton.edu/people/simon-levin), and [AJ te Velthuis](https://molbio.princeton.edu/people/aj-te-velthuis). I am pursuing the graduate certificate in Statistics and Machine Learning through the [Center for Statistics and Machine Learning](https://csml.princeton.edu/) (CSML), expected May 2027.

Before Princeton, I was a research assistant in the [Brook Lab](https://brooklab.org/) at the University of Chicago, working with Dr. Cara Brook on dengue dynamics, SARS-CoV-2, and bat-related work. I also contributed to national dengue surveillance analyses with the [Manning group at NIAID ICER Cambodia](https://www.niaid.nih.gov/research/icer-cambodia), and held research roles in Kim Lab (University of Pennsylvania), where I built **computational genomics** and **phylogenetics** pipelines. I earned a B.S. in **Mathematics**, with minors in **Computer Science (Machine Learning track)** and **Bioinformatics**.
